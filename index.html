CepRapor Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 14, 2026

CepRapor respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when using the application.

Information We Collect

The application may access the following device information in order to generate diagnostic reports:

  • Device model
  • Android version
  • Manufacturer information
  • Battery information
  • Storage information
  • Network information
  • Camera information

All information is processed locally on your device unless otherwise required by third-party services.

Advertising

CepRapor uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Ad performance information
